Start Here

Grand Theft Auto VI launched worldwide on November 19, 2026, for PlayStation 5 (including PS5 Pro Enhanced) and Xbox Series X|S. It is sold at the PlayStation Store, Microsoft Store, Rockstar Games Store, and retailers. Standard Edition is $79.99; Ultimate Edition is $99.99; a $20 Ultimate Edition Upgrade is sold separately. In-game purchases are optional. Online features need a platform account.

Jason and Lucia

Jason Duval

Official PlayStation Blog bio: Jason grew up around grifters and crooks. After a stint in the Army trying to shake off his troubled teens, he found himself in the Keys working for local drug runners. Meeting Lucia could be the best or worst thing to ever happen to him.

Lucia Caminos

Official PlayStation Blog bio: Lucia’s father taught her to fight as soon as she could walk. Fighting for her family landed her in the Leonida Penitentiary. Sheer luck got her out, and she wants the good life her mom dreamed of since their days in Liberty City. She is prepared to take matters into her own hands.

When an easy score goes wrong, Jason and Lucia end up in a criminal conspiracy stretching across the state of Leonida and have to rely on each other if they want to make it out alive.

What to do first

    Facts worth knowing early

    • The story is five chapters. PowerPyx estimates 60–80 hours for the story and about 200 hours for the platinum / 100% route.
    • There is no mission replay. Keep a manual save at the start and end of each chapter (15 manual slots plus one autosave).
    • Free-roam continues after the credits. Nothing required for 100% completion is missable after the story.
    • One trophy may be missable: after Chapter 1’s Down to Earth, accept Lucia’s morning meditation at Jason’s house. See the 100% & Trophies tab.
    • Cheats only unlock after you finish the story, and turning one on disables saving and trophies for that session.
    • Ultimate Edition bonuses are threaded through the chapters, not dumped at the start. Jason’s Enduro, kayak, and Ganado Retro Build are Chapter 1 only — use them before that chapter ends. After Chapter 1 mission Coming Home, the main-menu Store shows each bonus and its unlock condition (PC Gamer).
    • Bank cash in BuckMe. Pocket cash can be lost when you die or get busted.
    • You can switch between Jason and Lucia with Hold D-Pad Right + R1 (PS5) or RB (Xbox). Free switching opens in Chapter 2.

    Ultimate Edition and Pre-Order

    Official store copy says Ultimate Edition bonuses are “threaded across all aspects of Jason and Lucia’s story, with new items uncovered behind each chapter.” Check owned items and unlock conditions in the in-game Store menu. Standard Edition owners can buy the $20 Ultimate Edition Upgrade at any time. Exclusive shops still appear on the map without Ultimate, but their doors stay locked until you upgrade.

    Vintage Vice City Pack (pre-order / early purchase)

    This pack is not Ultimate-exclusive. Take-Two and PlayStation said purchases of either edition made before November 20, 2026 include the Vintage Vice City Pack. Digital PlayStation purchases on or before November 19 also include one month of GTA+ (claim by March 31, 2027; the Ultimate Edition Upgrade alone is not eligible). Rockstar has not said whether the pack will be sold separately later.

    GTA+ in GTA VI (confirmed benefits)

    GTA+ is $7.99 a month and renews until canceled. Bonuses only work while the membership is active and can be turned off in the Account menu. The list below follows Dot Esports’ post-launch breakdown.

Region-to-county borders are best checked on the in-game map; this guide does not invent a full town list.

    Side Jobs and Strangers

    PowerPyx treats Strangers & Freaks as optional mission strands. Completing 8 strands unlocks the trophy. PowerPyx marks seven as counting: Dr. Merle, Freddie, Kirk, Kyle, Alvan, Yago, and Pilot Pete. (Some later roundups omit Alvan and say only six; this guide follows PowerPyx’s own marks.) The eighth counting strand is still unpublished — check the trophy counter before and after a finale. Next missions often take in-game days or even a later chapter to appear.     Collectibles

    PowerPyx’s Progress Database is the source for the counts below. Many individual pins are still being mapped four days after launch, so this tab tracks types and totals, plus how to collect them — not a made-up list of every card or flamingo. Use the in-game Progress menu and a map that cites IGN or PowerPyx for exact pins. 100% completion only needs part of most collections; the required numbers are on each card and in the 100% tab.

    1010 Mural locations (PowerPyx, Nov 22)

    Photograph all five, then use the clue to open Southside Storage. These are not required for 100% and do not count as WAiNK scans.

    Vehicles

    The Progress menu tracks 557 vehicles: 277 base models and 280 Standout vehicles in fixed locations. 100% completion only requires scanning any 200 of the 277 base models with the WAiNK app (hold the scan control shown on screen). Also scan boats and aircraft. Standout vehicles do not count toward that 200. This tab covers how vehicles work, Ultimate and pre-order cars, and Wyman’s Classic Car Collection. It does not invent a list of all 277 base models.

    Ultimate, pre-order, and GTA+ vehicles

    Wyman’s Classic Car Collection (Ultimate Edition)

    Push Square confirmed this starts after you collect the ’67 Vapid Dominator Buggy from Paradise Garage in Watson Bay in Chapter 2. Wyman texts you on WhatUp. Scan wrecks with WAiNK (track it under Exploration > Find Cars for Wyman), then pay a $1,500 mechanic fee in the BuckMe app. Register the finished car at a Pay ’n’ Spray for $100 or park it in a garage; otherwise it is sent back to Wyman’s Auto Salvage (set a waypoint under Vehicles). Push Square had locations for four cars as of November 22 and marked the page as a work in progress. A fifth finished car is widely reported by fan wikis as a gift after the four rebuilds, but that was not in Push Square’s guide yet, so it is marked unconfirmed here.

    Weapons

    PowerPyx lists 196 weapon entries in the Progress menu: 53 guns, 9 melee, 4 explosives, and 130 variants. 100% completion needs any 50 of the 66 standard-issue weapons (the guns, melee, and explosives). Holding a weapon once is enough — buying is optional. Expanded Ammu-Nation stores (the word “Expanded” appears when you highlight the map icon) sell the full inventory, including grenade and rocket launchers. Unique named variants also appear in display cases; counts from major guides were still being reconciled at launch (PowerPyx 130 variants; IGN had documented 127 plus two Ultimate variants).

    Properties and Money

    There are no buyable houses or passive-income businesses confirmed at launch. PowerPyx’s garage guide lists 13 garages: 8 purchasable (Little Boxes; $160,000 total), 4 story rewards, and 1 reward for photographing all five 1010 Murals. Buy the eight from the Vice City Real Estate phone app after Chapter 2’s Daily Driver. PowerPyx says garage slots can reach a maximum of 18 (16 from garages plus 2 personal vehicles left outside). GameSpot’s November 18 article, written under pre-release restrictions, missed the later story and mural garages and listed two higher prices; those disagreements are noted on the cards. Prefer the in-game app.

    Trophies and achievements

    Positive Mental Attitude is the only potentially missable award. After Chapter 1 mission Down to Earth, talk to Lucia in the kitchen in the morning, follow her to the balcony, and Accept the meditation. PowerPyx and GamesRadar both say it is not yet 100% clear whether that house meditation is required for the 18-spot trophy, but it is safer to accept it. Cheats disable trophies. Difficulty does not: PlayStation lists accessibility assists (adjustable challenge, simplified QTEs, game speed), and PowerPyx says trophies are not tied to a difficulty mode.

    Challenges

    There are 80 challenges in 8 categories of 10, all available from the start. Track them under Progress > Challenges. Completing any 50 unlocks One Last Thing; any 70 of 80 are required for 100% completion, so you can skip the hardest 10. Wording below follows PowerPyx. GamesRadar lists the same 80; one Vehicle challenge disagrees on the unit (see Throttled).

    Tips and Controls

    Controls (PlayStation 5 and Xbox)

    The table follows TheGamer’s post-launch controls guide (November 22, 2026). Basic movement is the usual GTA layout: left stick to move, the bottom face button to sprint or jump, the top face button to enter or exit a vehicle. The game also has Control Reminders and Tutorial Reminders in its accessibility options.

    Cheats

    There are 61 cheats in five categories. The Cheats menu appears only after you finish the story (Progress > Cheats). Progress toward unlocks counts during the story, but you cannot enter a code until the menu exists. Activating a cheat disables saving and trophies for the rest of that session — save first. To enter a code, open the phone with D-Pad Up. Polygon (Nov 21, PS5): hold L1 and R1, then enter the D-pad sequence (LB and RB assumed on Xbox). PowerPyx: hold L1/LB and press R1/RB, then enter the sequence. Follow the on-screen prompt if they differ. Codes marked Confirmed were published by Polygon and/or PowerPyx. Where those two guides disagree on an unlock condition, both are quoted — use the in-game Cheats menu.
